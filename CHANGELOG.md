# Changelog

## 2026-11-27.2
- "Save to Cooking" bookmark: captures a recipe from the page in your own browser, so it works on sites that block importing (Tesco, Waitrose…)
- Website import now tries every route (relays + reader service) instead of stopping at a blocked page, and explains what to do if all fail
- Pasted/page-text parsing skips nutrition, comments and "you may also like" sections and keeps "For the …" sub-headings

## 2026-11-27.1
- First release: recipes with origins, comments, tags, rating and favourites; import from website / pasted text / page source / file; search, tag filter and sorting; servings scaling; shopping list; weekly menu planner; conversions (weight, volume, oven/gas mark, cups→grams, tables); backup, restore and export; installable PWA with offline support and Android share target.
